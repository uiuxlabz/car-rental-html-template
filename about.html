Our Story

Driving Excellence Since 2009

What started as a single office with 10 cars has grown into one of the most trusted car rental services in the region. Cental was founded with a simple mission: to make quality car rental accessible, affordable, and hassle-free for everyone.

Over the years, we have expanded our fleet to over 200 vehicles, opened 12 convenient locations, and served more than 15,000 satisfied customers. Our commitment to excellence has earned us over 35 industry awards and the trust of thousands.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, affordable, and premium car rental services that exceed expectations.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and customer-centric car rental brand in every city we serve.
